## Art direction and prompt

Create a premium, monumental GitHub hero for REFLEX, semiconductor process intelligence by doublezero. A physically credible silicon wafer in a precision metrology instrument; regular die grid, brushed titanium, grazing white light, subtle amber and cobalt interference. Charcoal negative space, restrained industrial research aesthetic, large legible white Swiss-style typography. Exact text: "doublezero", "REFLEX", "Semiconductor", "Process Intelligence", "Predict. Measure. Adapt." Keep generous safe margins. Avoid robots, brains, neural-network dots, HUDs, floating UI, fake charts, benchmark figures, third-party logos and deployment claims.
