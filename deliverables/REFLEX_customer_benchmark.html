Our focused customer hypothesis

One chamber. A real feedback delay.

Start with process and metrology engineers, or an equipment OEM, on one deposition or etch process. Replay real measurement timestamps after a process change; validate in shadow mode.

Maintenance recovery, these specific processes and customer willingness to pay have not been validated with REFLEX.

What we built

GRAPE baseline + timestamped residual filter + bounded Bayesian regime model. Store each requested sample's original prediction. When its label arrives, compare against that stored prediction and update the expert weights. Unarrived labels cannot enter the model.

Predict at t→Store & request→Label at t + delay→Update at arrival

Fixed sample-count delays; ordered arrivals. Raw sensor feature extraction, variable/out-of-order latency, process control and cross-chamber learning remain future work. No new base-algorithm claim.

03 / From research to an economic outcome

Sell a measured operating improvement.

Land: one process owner

Hypothesis: a process/metrology lead or equipment OEM wants fewer unreliable predictions while results are delayed. Begin with paid data integration and a fresh chronological evaluation.

Pilot hypothesis: ₩5–10M.
Integration hypothesis: ₩20–50M.
Pricing is unvalidated; no paid REFLEX customer.

Prove: an operational gate

Agree the physical tolerance and false-negative ceiling first. Then measure actual requests, post-maintenance recovery, engineer hours and marginal measurement costs on a fresh stream.

Only reduce sampling after the quality gate passes. Start in shadow mode, with existing process decisions intact.

Expand: learning across chambers

Vision: retain useful process experience, adapt across chambers, and shorten stabilization when materials or recipes change. Package a local runtime through equipment partners.

Cross-chamber transfer and faster next-generation process development are future goals. Customer data access and demonstrated transfer would be advantages; no moat established yet.

Methods and limitations

Frozen V7 weights. Two public, anonymized exported-feature datasets; delay 5 and 20 samples; sampling periods 1, 2, 5, 10 and 20; all offsets; no condition discarded. The customer protocol and development thresholds precede this evaluation, but the evaluation streams were used in earlier model development. Error threshold = GRAPE period-5 development absolute-error 95th percentile, separately for each dataset and delay.

Column-order semantics in upstream exported data remain unresolved. We consistently retain the same exported order; this is not a complete authors' benchmark reproduction. No real maintenance labels, physical quality specifications, actual elapsed instrument delays, fab costs, defect labels or yield were observed. The Bayesian posterior is capped at 32 hypotheses; total UI history is not constant-memory.
